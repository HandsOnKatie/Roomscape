# Ocean Depths — art generation prompts

This pack ships one tiny placeholder still (`scenes/depths_pano.png`, a generated
gradient) so the loader has something real to put on the wall out of the box.
Replace it — and add the two missing files below — with your own generations;
`GET /api/themes` lists exactly which files a pack still needs.

**scenes/depths_pano.png** (ships as a placeholder — regenerate as a still, or swap
in a video and change the filename in theme.json; panoramas span the wall, so
generate at wide aspect, e.g. 3× portrait width):
> Deep underwater scene, dark teal water fading to black below, gentle god rays from the surface far above, slow drifting particulate, distant silhouettes of a kelp forest, photoreal

**effects/bubbles.mp4** (not shipped — transparent-over-dark effect layer, portrait):
> Sparse small bubbles rising slowly against pure black background, soft focus, loopable

**sounds/underwater_loop.mp3** (not shipped): any CC0 underwater ambience loop
(e.g. Freesound, filter by CC0).
